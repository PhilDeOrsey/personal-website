About

My day job is software at Amplify — the rest of the site covers that. This page is everything else.

I’m generally happiest when I’m making something or poking at how something works. That shows up in a handful of hobbies.

In the kitchen

I enjoy cooking and am always exploring new vegetarian recipes. I've been making pizzas for a while and love the opportunity to get outside and make them over a live fire. I've recently gotten into making pasta, it's rewarding to make, and much easier with the right tools! Always looking to find that recipe that I can make from only things I've grown.

In the garden

I've been growing a garden for the past few years. I've had pretty good success growing things like tomatoes, cucumbers, beans, zucchini, lettuce, herbs, and potatoes. I've struggled with broccoli and peppers, maybe this is the year! I also have a few apple trees and berry bushes. Working on that edible food forest.

In the shop

I enjoy building physical things when I have the time. Over the years I have done a variety of 3D printing projects with students. I am waiting for my daughters to get old enough to get one of my own so we can explore. I also have a pen plotter and make some artwork with it, check out how I brought that into the classroom! I recently built a mud kitchen for my kids, definitely one of those "I can build that" moments. Of course, always keeping up with the house and fixing things that inevitably break.

Outside

We have a camper and like to go explore around New England and the surrounding states. It's great to get outside with everyone and spent that time away an in nature. We try to hike when we get the opportunity, its getting easier to go again as the kids get older. I always have a bunch of firewood to burn in my woodstove, or have fires outside to either cook on or just lounge around.

Gaming

I also enjoy gaming, playing video and boardgames. I played magic the gathering for many years and even competed on the pro tour! Recently I have been playing a lot of minecraft with my daughter and introducing her to new games. If you are ever looking for good games for young kids, Peaceable Kingdom has plenty of good offerings.

Music

I have played trumpet since I was very small. I think I first started when I was probably 5 years old. In high school I played in the Massachusetts all state band, won a MICCA gold medal with our high school band, and was on scholarship to play trumpet at Marietta College. I ended up with a minor in music but only a few credits short of a double major with Math. After my undergraduate work trumpet took a backseat to my graduate work but I was able to jump back in again when I played with the Westfield State Jazz Band a few years ago.
